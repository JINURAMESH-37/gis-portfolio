Selected Projects

01 | Landslide Susceptibility Mapping

Study Area: Wayanad, Kerala

Developed a GIS-based landslide susceptibility assessment using ArcGIS and ArcGIS Pro to identify landslide-prone regions through spatial analysis and machine learning.


Key Contributions

  • Prepared thematic maps of slope, aspect, curvature, elevation, geology, soil, rainfall, NDVI and LULC.
  • Generated drainage density, flow accumulation, stream power index and lineament density maps.
  • Conducted proximity analysis for roads, quarries, drainage networks and critical infrastructure.
  • Applied Random Forest, GBT and GLR models using ArcGIS Pro.
  • Compared model outputs to identify landslide susceptibility zones.

Tools: ArcGIS | ArcGIS Pro

02 | Groundwater Hydrogeochemical Assessment

Study Area: Kattumannarkoil Taluk, Tamil Nadu

Studied groundwater hydrogeochemical characteristics and water quality using groundwater samples and analytical parameters to assess drinking and irrigation suitability.


Focus: Groundwater quality, hydrogeochemistry and environmental assessment.
